Support

BrinaMaps by Sabrina Palmer

Email sabrinampalmer@gmail.com with what happened, where (a place name or the link from the Share button) and your iPhone model. Replies usually come within a few days.

Directions stop when the screen locks

Open your iPhone's Settings, find the app, tap Location and choose "Always" or "While Using the App" with Precise Location on. Check that Low Power Mode is off during long drives.

No voice directions

Turn the volume up while directions are running and check that voice is not set to Off in the app's trip options. Voice plays even with the ring/silent switch on silent.

A place or road is wrong or missing

The map comes from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can correct. Use "Report a map problem" in the app's settings, or email the place and what is wrong.

Premium

Premium adds live traffic. If you bought it and it is not on, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
