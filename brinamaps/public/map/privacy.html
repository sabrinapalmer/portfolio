Privacy policy

BrinaMaps by Sabrina Palmer · Effective October 26, 2026

BrinaMaps has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not sell or share information about you. Your saved places, recent searches and settings stay on your device.

The app's server passes these requests on and returns the answers. It does not store them, and it does not keep a record of who asked. Like any web server, the hosting provider (Vercel) may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability. Any service the app talks to directly (OpenFreeMap, Google Fonts, Wikipedia, Wikidata, the US Census Bureau and US Customs and Border Protection) can see your device's IP address, as with any website.

On the website version at brinamaps.com only, search and directions can also fall back to free community OpenStreetMap services (Photon, Nominatim and the FOSSGIS routing servers) when the main service is busy. The iPhone app does not use them.

The services above have their own privacy policies: Geoapify, TomTom, Vercel, OpenFreeMap, Google, US Census Bureau, Esri, Wikimedia.

Location

The app uses your location to show where you are, rank search results and give directions. While directions are running, it keeps using your location with the screen locked so it can keep guiding you; iOS shows a location indicator while this happens. Your location history is not stored or uploaded. You can turn location off in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. To switch traffic on, the app sends Apple's signed record of the subscription (which product, when it renews, and an anonymous purchase number Apple assigns) to the app's server, which checks it and replies with a pass kept on your device. The server does not store the record, and it never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. Manage or cancel Premium in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Your data and deleting it

There is no account, so there is nothing to delete on a server. Saved places, recent searches, settings and offline maps are stored only in the app on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it. Recent searches can also be cleared in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
