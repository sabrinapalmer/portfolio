Before I arrive

Fifteen minutes of prep is worth a thousand dollars of staging.

Lights on, fans off

Every lamp and overhead light on, ceiling fans stopped, blinds fully open or fully up.

Counters clear

Kitchen and bathroom counters empty except one or two decor items. Hide bins, cords, chargers and toiletries.

Beds made, towels folded

Hotel-style. Pillows plumped, throws straight, closet doors shut.

Outside too

Pool clean, cushions out, hose and cars out of frame. Patio furniture set as if guests are about to sit down.

I shoot a Canon 5D Mark IV with a 16–35mm, bracketed and blended so windows show the view instead of blowing out. Verticals corrected, colors neutral, no cartoon HDR. Portfolio at sabrinapalmer.com.
