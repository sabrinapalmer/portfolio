      Wildfires

      Active US wildfire areas from the national fire agencies (NIFC), updated a few times a day. Routes keep at least half a mile from fire areas that are not fully contained. This does not include evacuation zones or road closures: follow local officials.

          Saved places

            Offline map

            Places you have looked at stay viewable without a connection for a while. To be sure of an area, move the map to it and save it. Search and new routes still need a connection; a route you already have keeps working.

            More reliable search (optional)

            Search uses free public servers that are sometimes slow or down. A free Geoapify key (no card) makes it steadier: create one at myprojects.geoapify.com and paste it here. It stays on this device. With a key, what you type in search is sent to Geoapify.

            Premium: live traffic

            Routes that steer around jams, arrival times that include traffic, and traffic colours on the map. Everything else in the app is free.

            Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Privacy policy · Terms of use

            Live traffic (optional)

            Traffic needs a free TomTom key (no card): create one at developer.tomtom.com and paste it here. It stays on this device. With a key, driving routes come from TomTom and steer around jams, and your trip's start and end are sent to TomTom. Walking and biking are unchanged.

            About

            No ads, no sponsored places, no account. Your places and settings stay on this device. Maps and roads come from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can correct.
