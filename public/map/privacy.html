Privacy policy

BrinaMaps by Sabrina Palmer · Effective October 23, 2026

BrinaMaps has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not sell or share information about you. Your saved places, recent searches and settings stay on your device.

What leaves your device, and why

Some things need a server to answer. When you use them, only what that feature needs is sent:

When youWhat is sentTo
Search for a place or addressWhat you type and your approximate location, so nearby results come firstGeoapify, through the app's server at sabrinapalmer.com. When Geoapify has no matching business, the same search text and a rough location (about 1 km) go to TomTom, also through the app's server
Cross the US–Mexico border on a routeNothing about you: the app downloads the public wait-time listUS Customs and Border Protection, through the app's server
Get directions, or use "meet in the middle"The start, stops and destination of the trip, and your route settingsGeoapify, through the app's server
Use Premium trafficThe start, stops and destination of the trip, and the map area on screen for traffic coloursTomTom, through the app's server
Have wildfire avoidance onThe rough area of your tripThe US National Interagency Fire Center's public data service, and NASA's fire satellite service (FIRMS) through the app's server
Hit a problem in the app (a script error, or a search or route that fails)A short description of the error, the app version and the iPhone or browser version. No location, search text or anything about youThe app's server log
Look at the mapWhich map tiles to load (the area on screen)OpenFreeMap

The app's server passes these requests on and returns the answers. It does not store them, and it does not keep a record of who asked. Like any web server, the hosting provider (Vercel) may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

The services above have their own privacy policies: Geoapify, TomTom, Vercel, OpenFreeMap.

Location

The app uses your location to show where you are, rank search results and give directions. While directions are running, it keeps using your location with the screen locked so it can keep guiding you; iOS shows a location indicator while this happens. Your location history is not stored or uploaded. You can turn location off in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. To switch traffic on, the app sends Apple's signed record of the subscription (which product, when it renews, and an anonymous purchase number Apple assigns) to the app's server, which checks it and replies with a pass kept on your device. The server does not store the record, and it never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details. Manage or cancel Premium in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
