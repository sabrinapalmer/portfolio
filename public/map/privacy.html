Privacy policy

Map app by Sabrina Palmer · Effective October 23, 2026

The map has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not sell or share information about you. Your saved places, recent searches and settings stay on your device.

What leaves your device, and why

Some things need a server to answer. When you use them, only what that feature needs is sent:

When youWhat is sentTo
Search for a place or addressWhat you type and your approximate location, so nearby results come firstGeoapify, through the app's server at sabrinapalmer.com
Get directions, or use "meet in the middle"The start, stops and destination of the trip, and your route settingsGeoapify, through the app's server
Use Premium trafficThe start, stops and destination of the trip, and the map area on screen for traffic coloursTomTom, through the app's server
Have wildfire avoidance onThe rough area of your tripThe US National Interagency Fire Center's public data service
Look at the mapWhich map tiles to load (the area on screen)OpenFreeMap

The app's server passes these requests on and returns the answers. It does not store them, and it does not keep a record of who asked. Like any web server, the hosting provider (Vercel) may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

The services above have their own privacy policies: Geoapify, TomTom, Vercel, OpenFreeMap.

Location

The app uses your location to show where you are, rank search results and give directions. While directions are running, it keeps using your location with the screen locked so it can keep guiding you; iOS shows a location indicator while this happens. Your location history is not stored or uploaded. You can turn location off in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the app only learns whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
