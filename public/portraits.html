What to expect

Fast, unposed, and done before anyone gets bored.

We meet on the beach at La Salina (exact spot sent with your confirmation). I shoot a Canon 5D Mark IV and work quickly; kids and dogs do best when nobody has to hold still. For dog sessions, bring a favorite toy and a long leash; I photograph them running, swimming and shaking off, then clone the leash out.
