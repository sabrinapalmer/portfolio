Music Analyst

Your listening history and playlists, outside the app. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop your Spotify export here The ZIP as downloaded, or the JSON files inside it. Account data export, extended streaming history, or both.
How to get the export
  1. Spotify account page → Privacy settings → Download your data.
  2. Tick Account data (playlists, library, last year of history, ~5 days) and Extended streaming history (every play since your account started, up to ~30 days).
  3. Spotify emails a ZIP. Drop it here. The page reads StreamingHistory*.json, Streaming_History_Audio_*.json, Playlist*.json, YourLibrary.json and Follow.json; everything else is ignored.

Move to another service

Transfer tools (Soundiiz, TuneMyMusic, FreeYourMusic) and most apps accept a CSV with Title, Artist, Album columns. These files use exactly that layout.

Apple Music / YouTube Music / Tidal / Deezer

Use the CSVs above with a transfer tool, or paste them into the service's importer where one exists. Local-file M3Us won't help here.

Set up your own music server

Navidrome, Jellyfin, Plexamp and Funkwhale all play files you own and import M3U playlists. The M3Us here reference files as Artist/Album/Title.ext, matching the folder layout beets, Lidarr and most taggers produce. Rename the extension to match your rips.

The shopping list is every unique album that appears in your playlists, liked songs, or top plays, with how many of your tracks it covers, so you can buy or rip the ones that matter first. Bandcamp and Discogs search links are included.
